Business Model

Two Booking Modes

Mode A: Whole Table

Groups book the table and host for multiple hours. Host-led gaming sessions with premium pricing.

Mode B: Open Seating

Individual seats sold per session at $15-40 price points. Lower entry barrier for casual players. Higher throughput per table.

Platform Role: Acts as a booking agent matching players to games, times, groups, and venues.

Phase 1: Launch (Target Sept 17, 2026)

Focus: Coordinate a specific marathon gaming weekend (Sept 18-20) with core team coordination features for 6 attendees.

Core Features

  • Multi-day event page with RSVP and share functionality
  • Schedule board showing games and time blocks
  • Potluck coordination board for meals
  • In-app group messaging (Supabase Realtime)
  • Game cards with rules links and teaching times
  • Logistics panel (parking, access, house rules)

Explicitly Cut

  • Payments processing (no Stripe)
  • Seating algorithms
  • Public-facing pages
  • Multi-table scheduling
  • Recurring bookings

Phase 2: Expansion (~6 months)

Paid Bookings & Monetization

  • Stripe payment integration
  • Insurance partnerships
  • Membership models (unlimited plays, monthly passes)
  • Add-ons: "host assistance" teaching services

Smart Features

  • Advanced seating algorithms
  • Skill/experience level matching
  • Teacher/facilitator matching
  • Review and rating system
  • Host verification and insurance

Phase 3: Scale

Expand beyond private events to:

  • Game shop partnerships (venue.kind = 'shop')
  • Convention bookings and tournament hosting
  • Multi-location support

Key Business Guardrails

These constraints shape all product decisions:

Childcare Licensing: Never position as childcare. Gaming entertainment only.
Overnight Stays: Trigger short-term rental regulations. Verify local zoning.
Food & Drink: Platform coordinates potluck signup only. Money never touches food/alcohol. Hosts handle all food safety.
Business Model Testing: Both hourly and per-seat models must be measured before committing to one.
